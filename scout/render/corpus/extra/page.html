Layout without a browser

HTML and CSS go in, boxes and pixels come out — no Chromium, no headless process, no 100 MB of RSS to decide where a paragraph breaks.

layout

Real geometry

Block flow, margin collapsing, line breaking and flex, all measured against Chromium's own numbers.

Every fixture in the corpus is diffed against a real browser, not against a guess.

A render engine is mostly bookkeeping. Margins collapse through open edges but are stopped by borders and padding. A line breaks when the next word no longer fits, and the break depends on the font, not on the string length. Text inside an inline element belongs to that element's box, which is the font's content area rather than the line box it sits in. Get any of those subtly wrong and the geometry still looks plausible while being a few pixels off everywhere.
